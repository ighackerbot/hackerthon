Where scholarship money quietly disappears

See exactly where your scholarship is stuck — and why.

On the real NSP, an application can sit silently at any of four verification desks with no explanation. That silence is where bribes, favoritism, and lost files happen. NSP Sunlight makes every step public, timestamped, and explained in plain language — so nothing can happen in the dark.

Today, on NSP

  • "Under process at institute level" — for weeks, no name attached
  • Rejections cite a code, never a reason a student can act on
  • No way to know if your college is unusually slow or unusually strict
  • Complaints go into a form that closes itself with no explanation

On Sunlight

  • Every stage names the officer, the timestamp, and the decision
  • Rejections come with a plain-language reason and next step
  • Institutes with abnormal rejection or delay rates are flagged, publicly
  • Grievances stay open and visible until resolved with a stated reason
